# Bridge Proof — 60-second pitch

“People learn by building, but a project description is difficult to turn into a portable, verifiable skill record.

Bridge Proof connects learning evidence to a structured credential on Solana. Our working prototype uses the official Solana Attestation Service, with no custom blockchain program or database.

Here is a real Devnet credential. Its assessment is clearly labeled as a deterministic demo, not a real AI evaluation. The optional OpenAI integration is implemented, but this demonstration makes no paid AI calls.

We can open its real transaction, retrieve the credential in another browser, and verify the issuer, schema, signer, expiry, and data integrity directly from chain state. We can also compare the original synthetic evidence with its SHA-256 hash.

The public site is read-only and holds no private key. Verification proves the issuer and integrity of the record—not learner identity or competence.

Bridge Proof makes skill credentials portable, inspectable, and independently verifiable.”
